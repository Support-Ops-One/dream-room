## Prompt 1 — Design a small experiment

```text
I'm exploring a problem and want your help designing one small experiment to better understand it.

Act as my thinking partner. Guide me through the following three questions, asking only ONE question at a time and waiting for my answer before continuing:

1. What problem are you exploring, and who is experiencing it?

2. What have you actually observed or learned about the problem so far?

3. What are you still unsure about, or what assumptions do you need to test?

After I've answered all three questions, suggest ONE small, realistic experiment I could try this week to learn more about the problem.

Explain what I would do, what I might learn, and one limitation of the experiment.

Keep your language simple and conversational. Don't jump to solutions, invent facts, or ask extra questions. Start by asking me question one.
```
